# Max-level skill artwork and future gameplay notes

User requested asset design first. Do not implement evolution rules as part of this art task.

## Rasengan → Rasenshuriken (Wind Release)
- Future trigger: Rasengan reaches max level, then player selects Chakra Focus.
- Future behavior: throw a wind shuriken; attract enemies along its flight path; at the end of its range, detonate with a wind pressure shockwave.
- This task supplies flight, pressure burst/dissipation and a separate ground shockwave sprite sequence. Suction, final-range detonation, new damage and evolution availability are NOT implemented.
- Preserve the approved deep-blue spiral orb identity; add four white/cyan wind blades and fast orbiting wind bands.
- Ground shockwave is a top-down elliptical ring on its own transparent layer so it can render below characters.

## Other max-level variants
- Taijutsu: same blunt pressure silhouette, warmer gold/white core and cyan secondary air ring; no sword/kunai visuals on Naruto.
- Shadow shuriken: same four metal blades and center hole, violet/blue energy edges and circular white wind trail.
- Paper tag: same paper silhouette and red seal, violet/gold seal pulse; detonation retains flash → fire ring → smoke, with a hotter gold/cyan energy accent.
- Twin Slash: archive for a future character; do not bind it to Naruto.
